Step 04 — the one that bites

Use your real date of birth.

Not a convenient one. A birthday you invent cannot be defended later — and on YouTube the thing that challenges it doesn't read the birthday at all.

YouTube

13–17 → uploads default to Private. 18+ → Public.

A wrong birthday means every upload silently lands private and you find out from the view count.

Instagram

13–17 → Teen Account, private by default.

Content is excluded from Explore and hashtags entirely. That is a growth channel that cannot grow.

YouTube

Age estimation runs regardless of the birthday on the account.

Their words: “We do this regardless of the birthdate in their account.” Clearing a wrong flag needs government ID, credit card, or selfie — none of which will match a birthday you invented. A locked Google account takes the YouTube channel with it.

After signup

What happens once the accounts exist.

  1. One story, one video, both platforms. Never three cuts of the same story on one channel — that is exactly what YouTube's "Reused content" and Meta's "Duplicative content" triggers name, and a new account has no credit to spend on it.
  2. Three opening variants, rotated across stories. V1 is the best-scoring opening frame, V2 the second-best with the same line, V3 the best frame with a rewritten opening line. V1 vs V2 tells us whether the frame matters; V1 vs V3 tells us whether the line does.
  3. 1–2 posts a day, ramping. Thirty-six uploads onto a week-old channel is a spam signal on its own, and it would poison the numbers we're collecting.
  4. Winner = CTR × average view percentage — percentage, not seconds, because the videos run 60 to 77 seconds and a raw seconds comparison quietly rewards the short ones.
One thing OAuth won't fix. The YouTube Analytics API has no thumbnail impressions and no impression CTR — it carries adImpressions, which is advertising, and cpm. Those two numbers live only in Studio's Reach tab. So the API can pull the retention half automatically, and the CTR half stays a human read — two numbers typed per post, not six.
